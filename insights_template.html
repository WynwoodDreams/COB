            How these numbers are made

            • Everything here is counted from the listings on the board at the time it was last updated. No survey, no outside data. The board keeps postings for 90 days, so this is a 90-day window.
            • Pay counts internships that quote an hourly figure. A range counts as its midpoint; yearly or monthly figures and entry-level jobs are left out. A group needs 5 hourly listings to get a bar.
            • New compares the last 14 days with the 14 before. Up or down by 15% or more moves the reading.
            • Skills are counted once per listing, from a fixed list, so a posting that says "Excel" three times counts once.
            • These describe what employers published, not what every intern earns or what every role needs.
