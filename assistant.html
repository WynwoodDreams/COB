How it works. Tell it what you study or can do, where in South Florida you are, and when you are free. It filters the board's own listings by location and eligibility, then an AI model ranks the closest ones and explains the fit. It only ever shows listings that are on the board, with the board's own apply links, and it cannot tell whether a posting is still open: check the employer's page before you count on one.

Describe yourself in a sentence or two.

Interests, skills, experience so far, where you are and when you can work. It will ask at most three short questions if something important is missing, then show up to five listings with why each fits and what the posting asks for that you have not mentioned.

  • Required and preferred qualifications are read from each posting's own bullets.
  • Ask follow-up questions about the listings it finds; it answers from their text only.
  • Nothing you type is stored on the server; the conversation lives in this tab.
